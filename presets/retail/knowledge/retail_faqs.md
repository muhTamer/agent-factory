# Retail FAQs

## Returns & Refunds
**What is the return policy for unopened items?**  
You can return new and unopened products within 365 days with proof of purchase for a full refund.

**What is the return policy for opened items?**  
Opened products may be returned within 180 days with proof of purchase, provided the item is clean, undamaged, and complete.

**What items cannot be returned?**  
Plants, cut fabric, custom countertops, and as-is products are not eligible for return.

**How is my refund issued?**  
Refunds are made to the original payment method. In-store refunds take 3-10 business days; online return refunds take approximately 2 weeks.

## Orders & Delivery
**How can I track my order?**  
Use the Track and Manage My Order tool with your order number and email or phone number.

**What delivery options are available?**  
Small parcel shipping (FedEx/UPS), Standard truck delivery, Scheduled Doorstep, Scheduled In-Home, and Express In-Home delivery.

**Does the store offer free shipping?**  
Loyalty members receive savings on select delivery options. Small order shipping is free for members on qualifying orders of $50 or more.

## Complaints & Support
**What if my product arrived damaged?**  
Visit the store with the damaged product and your receipt, or contact Customer Support for the quickest resolution.

**What if I'm missing parts?**  
Missing hardware can be ordered free of charge via the spare parts portal or by calling Customer Support.

**How do I contact Customer Support?**  
Customer Support is available Sunday-Saturday, 8:00 AM to 12:00 AM ET, via phone or online chat.
